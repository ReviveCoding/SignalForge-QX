# Publication boundaries

This repository is a **public research engineering snapshot** of SignalForge-QX through v3.8. It is not a complete internal experiment archive, an independently qualified economic backtest, or a trading system.

## Supported research claims

- 120 genuine CUDA SIA mature OOF fits and exact saved-checkpoint prediction replay.
- 12,480 aligned five-family retrospective development forecast rows, with 98 passing scoped v3.8 tests.
- Main-A 2020: SIA normalized pinball loss (NPL) was 1.134% lower than the strongest I0 baseline.
- Nested-B 2022: SIA NPL was 15.13% lower than the older RGMF but 0.493% higher than strongest I0.
- Both BAR and additional post-hoc calibration failed stated performance-promotion criteria and were not promoted.
- 4,737 maturity-aware historical *test-only* monitoring replay rows and error/regime/seed/challenger analyses.
- Hypothetical long-only P0 portfolio weight differences, not actual returns.

## Not supported

- First-publication timestamp certified Tier-A historical source versions: **0**.
- Complete P1 price/dividend/split/cash records qualifying real portfolio economics: **0 / 27,088 sessions**.
- Independent governance review, operational freeze, genuinely new forward forecasts or mature future outcomes: **0**.
- Live alpha, Sharpe ratio, actual orders, market execution, regulatory model approval, or guaranteed false-positive rates.
- Statistically confirmatory predictive superiority across all baselines and tracks.

## What was excluded

Private/downloaded vendor historical data, unredacted user account paths, credentials, internal run ledgers, checkpoint binaries, exact raw prediction/target panels, private artifact caches, and all Codex/LLM prompts. Public source files containing a local machine username were copied with a generic **USERNAME** placeholder, meaning this source snapshot is **not byte-identical** to the internally executed, hashed code.

The published technical reports and aggregate CSVs provide traceable descriptive results, not a standalone fully reproducible research dataset. Some source data may require licensing and time-version authentication. No open-source license grant accompanies this repository.
